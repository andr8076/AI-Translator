# Little House on the Prairie — established Danish terms and notes
# Lines are inserted into the model prompt; apply only when the context matches.
Walnut Grove => Valnøddal
Half-Pint => Lille Laura
Meadowlark => stær
blind school => blindeskolen
“meet your stage” means “leve op til dine forventninger” when it is used idiomatically.
“high time” should be translated by its meaning (often “det er længe siden”), not literally as “høj tid”.
“holding out hope” means “holde fast i håbet” or “give håb”, depending on the speaker and sentence.
Bracketed stage directions such as [Birds chirping] and [Sighs] are translated into Danish while the brackets are retained.
“How old are you?” => “Hvor gammel er du?”; never use “hvor ældre er du” for an age question.
“I know, don’t apologize.” => “Jeg ved det, du behøver ikke undskylde.”
“combed his hair” => “kæmmede sit hår”; do not translate “combed” as “kæmpede”.
“wagon wheel” in the song is naturally “et hjul” in this subtitle context.
